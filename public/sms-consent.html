SMS Messaging Consent

Last updated: April 9, 2026

ShopDesk uses SMS messaging to keep business owners and their customers informed about appointments, booking activity, and urgent business alerts. This page explains what messages we send, how we collect consent, and how to opt out. This page is provided to satisfy carrier registration requirements under TCR/Twilio 10DLC guidelines.

What Messages We Send

ShopDesk sends the following types of SMS messages:

  • Appointment confirmations, Sent to customers immediately after a booking is made via our AI receptionist
  • Appointment reminders, Sent to customers 24 hours before their scheduled appointment
  • Booking alerts, Sent to business owners and staff when a new appointment is booked
  • Missed call follow-up, Sent to callers who called but did not complete a booking
  • Emergency notifications, Sent to the designated emergency contact when a caller reports an urgent situation, such as a same-day service issue, access problem, or other caller-reported emergency

Consent Statement

The following consent applies to all SMS communications from ShopDesk:

By providing your phone number to ShopDesk, you agree to receive SMS messages including appointment confirmations, reminders, and business alerts that you specifically opted in to receive. Message frequency varies based on booking activity. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help.

Below is a sample of the opt-in checkbox presented to business owners during account setup. Providing an owner mobile number and agreeing to receive owner alerts are both optional.

I agree to receive SMS booking alerts and urgent business notifications at this number. Consent is optional. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help. See our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

How SMS Consent Works, Step by Step

The following describes the exact consent collection flow for every customer booking made through ShopDesk:

  • 1
    Customer calls the business. The call is answered by the ShopDesk AI receptionist on behalf of the business.
  • 2
    ShopDesk AI receptionist answers and assists the customer with scheduling an appointment, collecting name, service details, service type, and preferred time.
  • 3
    AI asks for the customer's mobile number for the confirmation text: "What is the best phone number to send your confirmation to?"
  • 4
    Customer verbally provides their mobile number. The AI repeats it back to confirm accuracy.
  • 5
    AI reads the consent disclosure aloud, word for word: "Got it. You will receive a confirmation text at that number shortly. By providing your number, you agree to receive appointment confirmation and reminder texts from [Business Name]. Msg and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out."
  • 6
    Customer verbally confirms or does not object. Absence of objection after the disclosure is treated as consent. The booking is finalized and consent is recorded.
  • 7
    ShopDesk logs the consent record in our secure database, including: phone number, consent timestamp, call ID, appointment ID, business ID, and opt-in source = "phone_call_verbal".

How We Collect Consent

Customers (Callers)

When a customer books an appointment via our AI receptionist, the AI verbally collects the customer's phone number and reads the consent disclosure aloud before any text is sent. The exact language used is:

"Got it. You will receive a confirmation text at that number shortly. By providing your number, you agree to receive appointment confirmation and reminder texts from [Business Name]. Msg and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out."

Consent is recorded at the time of booking confirmation along with the call ID and timestamp.

Shop Owners & Staff

During account setup at app.shopdesk.site, business owners can optionally provide a mobile number and optionally check a box agreeing to receive booking alerts and emergency notifications. If no number is provided or the box is left unchecked, onboarding still continues and no owner-alert SMS messages are sent.

Consent Logging

ShopDesk logs the following data at the time of consent for every customer who provides a phone number during a call:

  • Phone number, the number provided verbally by the customer
  • Timestamp, the exact date and time consent was recorded (UTC)
  • Call ID, the unique identifier for the phone call in which consent was given
  • Appointment ID, the booking record linked to this consent
  • Business ID, the business on whose behalf the number was collected
  • Opt-in source, recorded as "phone_call_verbal" for all call-based bookings

This record is retained for compliance purposes and is available upon request for carrier or regulatory review.

TCPA Compliance Statement

ShopDesk supports compliance with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) by:

  • Collecting verbal consent during the phone call booking flow, before any message is sent
  • Clearly identifying the business by name in every message sent to customers
  • Disclosing that message and data rates may apply in the consent language and in every message
  • Providing opt-out instructions (Reply STOP) in every outbound message
  • Honoring STOP requests immediately, no further messages are sent after an opt-out
  • Logging consent with a timestamp and call ID to provide an auditable record

ShopDesk does not send marketing or promotional messages. All messages are transactional and directly related to booked appointments or urgent business alerts.

Message Types and Frequency

ShopDesk sends only transactional messages. Message types are:

  • Appointment confirmation, sent immediately after booking is complete
  • 24-hour reminder, sent the day before the scheduled appointment
  • Missed call follow-up, sent if a customer called but did not complete a booking
  • Emergency alert to business owner, sent if the caller reports a urgent service issue

Frequency: Transactional only. Typically 1-3 messages per appointment. No marketing or promotional messages are ever sent.

Opt-Out Instructions

You can stop receiving SMS messages from ShopDesk at any time:

  • Reply STOP to any ShopDesk SMS message to unsubscribe immediately
  • Reply HELP to any message to receive support information
  • Contact us directly at slarve@shopdesk.site to request removal

After opting out, you will receive one final confirmation message and no further SMS communications will be sent.
